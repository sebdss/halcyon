01 About

Halcyon, the kingfisher: perfectly still above the water, then one clean move.

A launcher, not a store.

Halcyon sells nothing and doesn't replace your accounts. It reads the games your platforms have already installed, the ones you keep in your own folders and your ROMs, and brings them together in one single library.

Games launch through their platform when it requires it; Halcyon keeps everything else in one place: playtime, achievements, saves, mods and launch profiles. Written in Rust with a Slint interface, it runs natively, with no embedded browser. Online features (artwork, metadata, achievements, updates) are clearly marked and can be turned off.

  • One unified library

    A game that disappears from a source is marked “not installed”, never deleted: favorites, playtime and settings stay.

  • All your platforms

    Steam, Epic Games through its launcher, Heroic or Legendary, GOG through Heroic or GOG Galaxy, game folders, manual additions and emulators.

  • Built for playing

    Controller support across the whole desktop app, a console mode for the TV, universal search with Ctrl K.

  • Smooth

    Hardware-accelerated native rendering, selection kept from one view to the next, adjustable motion: full, reduced or none.

  • Light and fast

    Performance mode turns off effects and background tasks while you play, by hand or on its own.

  • Its own identity

    Two colors, each with one job: blue shows where you are, orange is the move to play.

02 Features

Everything around your games, in one place.

Halcyon does more than launch: it organizes, tracks, backs up and measures, without ever touching your games.

Library

Five views, one selection.

Your keyboard or controller selection is kept from one view to the next. The index filters by source, collection and genre; smart collections fill themselves according to your rules.

  • Grid
  • List
  • Carousel
  • Immersive
  • Compact

Search forgives typos and understands acronyms and filters:

dev: genre: playtime:>10h achievements:missing update:yes
Steam
All libraries, local playtime, owned games that aren't installed
Epic Games
Epic launcher on Windows, Heroic and Legendary on Windows and Linux
GOG
Games installed by Heroic or by GOG Galaxy (Windows)
Your folders
Native Windows and Linux executables, manual additions (.exe, .lnk, .sh, AppImage…)
Emulation
ROMs sorted by platform, multi-disc games

Game page and achievements

Every game, with its real numbers.

Playtime with its origin (platform, measured or corrected), last session, achievements, screenshots, saves, mods and launch settings. Your changes are never overwritten by a sync.

  • SteamWeb API keyUnlocks and rarity of every achievement
  • RetroAchievementsUsername and keyROMs recognized by hash, softcore and Hardcore modes, points
  • Standalone gamesCustomAchievements you create, marked “unverified”
  • Epic GamesUnavailableNo public API: nothing is shown, nothing is estimated

Console mode

From desk to couch.

Designed for the TV and a controller: the selected game fills the screen, with a row of covers, a full-screen game page, an options menu for each game and a control center. Open it with the Console button or the F11 key, then drive it entirely with a controller (XInput on Windows, evdev on Linux) or the keyboard.

  • A Select
  • B Back
  • X Favorite
  • Select Game options
  • Start Control center
  • LBRB Section

Emulation

Your ROMs, organized like your PC games.

Detection in folders, the PATH and Flatpak, a default emulator and one per game, argument profiles. Definitions are JSON files: adding one takes no code.

  • DuckStation PlayStation
  • PPSSPP PSP
  • Dolphin GameCube, Wii
  • Cemu Wii U
  • Ryujinx Switch
  • melonDS Nintendo DS
  • mGBA Game Boy Advance

What works behind the scenes.

Your progress, kept safe.

Halcyon finds save locations, keeps versioned copies before and after every session, and can share them between several PCs.

Mods you can take back out.

Before deploying a mod, Halcyon copies the original files. Removing a mod puts the game back exactly as it was.

Adapters
Generic, BepInEx, Unreal paks
Management
Import, load order, conflicts, profiles
Removal
Complete, original files restored
Nexus Mods

Every game, launched your way.

A profile gathers everything around launching a game, and also applies when it's launched remotely.

Launch
Arguments, environment variables, resolution, display
Frame rate cap
Unity, Unreal, Source and Godot games
System
Preferred graphics card, process priority
Around the game
Scripts before and after, companion programs, mod and controller profiles

Measuring without touching anything.

CPU, memory, graphics cards and storage live, measured only while the page is open. Halcyon closes no programs and changes no system settings.

Per session
One reading every 10 seconds, summary kept, can be turned off
Frame rate
Average, 1% low and 0.1% low read from MangoHud or PresentMon logs, with no injection into games
Apps
The most demanding ones, grouped, Halcyon included
Recommendation
A single, reversible action offered: Performance mode

Always up to date, without thinking about it.

The new version is downloaded and verified in the background, then installed at the next start. If it doesn't start correctly, the previous one comes back on its own.

Channels
Stable or Beta, never changed without you
Verification
Ed25519-signed manifest and SHA-256 hashes
Downloads
Queue, pause, resume after a dropped connection, bandwidth limit
Your games
Steam, Epic and GOG keep updating them through their client; Halcyon shows what's pending

Playing from another room.

Halcyon drives Sunshine on the gaming PC and Moonlight on the device that displays, without reimplementing any streaming protocol.

Publishing
